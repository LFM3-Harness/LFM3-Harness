# Greenhouse watering

Tomatoes: water every 2 days, 1.5 liters per plant.
Basil: water every day, 0.5 liters per pot.
Cacti: water every 14 days, 0.2 liters per pot.
Ferns: keep soil moist; mist leaves on Mondays and Thursdays.

The water tank holds 20 liters. Refill it when it drops below 4 liters.
