# Coffee machine cleaning

Weekly cleaning (every Friday):
- Empty the bean hopper and wipe it with a dry cloth.
- Run the cleaning tablet cycle with 500 ml of water.
- Remove and rinse the drip tray.
- Descale the steam wand with the cleaning pin.

Monthly cleaning (first Monday):
- Replace the water filter.
- Clean the brew group with the brush.

Safety: unplug the machine before removing the brew group.
